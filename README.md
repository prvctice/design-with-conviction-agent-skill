# Design with Conviction

**A point of view for your AI agent's design work.**

AI agents have seen a huge amount of design, so on their own they drift toward the
average of it: clean, competent, and forgettable. This skill gives your agent a
designer's convictions instead. They're distilled from a century of design
manifestos, from William Morris and Adolf Loos through Dieter Rams, Massimo
Vignelli, Beatrice Warde, and Robert Venturi to *First Things First* and today's
repair and climate declarations.

It works alongside whatever your agent uses to build things (slides, web pages,
posters, app screens, documents, design systems). Those tools cover *how* to
build. This skill covers what to believe while building and what to check
before showing you anything.

## What it changes

- **It takes a side.** Before starting, the agent writes a four-line brief: who
  it's for, the stance and what it's against, the constraint it's imposing, and
  what content is real.
- **It subtracts.** Every element has to earn its place. Before anything is
  shown, the agent runs a subtraction pass, a "could this be for anyone?" check,
  an honesty check, an accessibility check, and a next-editor check.
- **It's honest.** No invented testimonials, logos, or metrics. Placeholders are
  clearly marked.
- **It recommends.** You get one strong recommendation and at most one genuinely
  different alternative, instead of five safe variations.
- **It respects your edits.** Ask for one change and you get one change.
- **It learns your taste.** A *House principles* section holds your own manifesto,
  and those principles outrank everything else.

## Try it

> "Design a landing page for my ceramics studio. Here's the copy and six photos."

> "Turn these meeting notes into a 10-slide deck for the board."

> "Critique this app screen. Be honest about what's generic."

> "Make a poster for a noise-music night. Take a real stance."

## Get started

**1. Ask your agent to install it.** Paste this into Claude Code, Codex, or
Gemini CLI:

> Install the Design with Conviction skill from
> https://github.com/prvctice/design-with-conviction-agent-skill.
> Read its setup instructions and install it for the agent I'm using. Preserve any
> existing customizations.

In the Claude app, you can save the contents of
[`SKILL.md`](skills/design-with-conviction/SKILL.md) as a skill.

**2. Make it yours.** Open `SKILL.md` and fill in **House principles**: a few
numbered lines about what you believe and a short list of things you never do.
Your agent will also offer to add a principle when the same feedback comes up
twice.

**3. Design something.** It loads for any design task. To be sure it's used, say
"use the design-with-conviction skill."
